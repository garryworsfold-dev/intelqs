About IntelQS LLC FZ

IntelQS LLC FZ is a UAE-based commercial and quantity surveying consultancy. With over 30 years of international experience across the UK, Ireland, Middle East, India and the USA, IntelQS provides high-integrity commercial, financial and operational support to construction and industrial clients.

Our focus is on precision, data-driven decision making and financial clarity. We help clients understand their true commercial position, manage risk, and protect margin through disciplined cost control, accurate forecasting and transparent reporting.

IntelQS operates from RAKEZ Business Zone, Ras Al Khaimah, UAE, serving both local and international clients with a commitment to integrity, professionalism and measurable results.
